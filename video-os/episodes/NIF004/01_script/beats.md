# NIF004 · BEAT SHEET

**Topic:** Google Maps — the hidden economics of the "free" map · **Pillar C** (The Middleman)
· **Pattern: The Meter** — one free map, three meters running behind it: every app pays per
look, every business pays to be the pin, and you pay in the trail you leave. Then the
reversal: you are the meter reading that prices the other two.
Companion to [script.md](script.md). 34 beats, B00–B33.

> superseded at Step 2 once the creator returns `B00.mp3 … B33.mp3` and real durations are reconciled
> (§10), exactly as NIF003's `EST` values were superseded by `reconcile.json`. Do not build anything in
> Remotion against the `EST` numbers below (§7 — "nothing is built against estimated durations").

texture only. Per runbook §22.8 (2026-09-07) NIF004 owes **2–3 genuinely new visible assets/mechanisms**
and **~3 distinct prop-locations** (locked field + 2–4 props; never a built room). Candidates below in the
